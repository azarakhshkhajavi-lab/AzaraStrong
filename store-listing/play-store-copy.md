# Google Play listing

## App name
Azara Strong

## Short description
Three focused home workouts for strength, posture, core and confidence.

## Full description
Stand taller and feel stronger with a realistic workout plan made for home.

Azara Strong organizes three focused 20-minute sessions using dumbbells and resistance bands:

- Posture + Back
- Core + Glutes
- Upper Body + Burn

Use the built-in countdown timer, check off each movement, and track your weekly consistency. Clear form cues help you train with control, while heartburn-aware notes provide more comfortable standing alternatives when appropriate.

Progress stays on your device. No account, advertisements, analytics, or internet connection is required.

Azara Strong provides general educational fitness guidance and is not a medical service or medical device.
