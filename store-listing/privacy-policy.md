# Privacy Policy — Azara Strong

Effective date: August 25, 2026

Azara Strong does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information.

Workout completion and streak information are stored only on the user's device using Android local preferences. The app does not require an account, does not contain advertising or analytics, and does not request internet access.

If the app is uninstalled, locally stored progress may be deleted. Android device backup or transfer features may copy local preferences when enabled by the user.

The app provides general educational fitness information and is not medical advice or a medical device.
